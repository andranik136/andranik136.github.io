Apple II Plus

May 2026
Blender

A tribute to vintage computing history, this project is a photorealistic 3D recreation of the classic 1979 Apple II Plus microcomputer.

Modeled and textured in Blender, the render captures every nuance of the classic personal computer—from the textured beige plastic enclosure and classic rainbow badge to the mechanical keycaps and monochrome CRT monitor display situated in a cozy desktop studio setting.
