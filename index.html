About Me

Hello! I’m Andranik. For over 10 years, I’ve been helping brands and businesses bring their digital ideas to life. I don’t just specialize in one field; I work across the entire creative spectrum—from front-end development to graphic design and 3D modeling.

I love the challenge of taking a complex concept and turning it into a polished, engaging experience. Using a mix of VueJS/JavaScript, Adobe Creative Suite, and Blender, I bridge the gap between technical functionality and visual storytelling.

Outside of my studio, you’ll find me catching the sunset on the coast or traveling with my dog, Archie. I’m always open to new opportunities and interesting conversations. Send me an email—I’d love to hear what you’re working on!
